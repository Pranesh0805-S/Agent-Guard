Every risky action passes through the guard first.
Three layers. Each one catches what the last one missed.
1 · AllowlistDeterministic. Sends outside the company domain are stopped. No model call.
2 · Blind reviewerA second model call sees only your goal and the action, never the email body.
3 · Approval queueAnything blocked waits for a human to approve or deny.
Why a blind reviewer?
The attacker's text can't argue with a reviewer that never reads it.
